Suzana Sampeur is a South Florida real estate professional with LUXE Properties, specializing in residential sales, first-time homeownership, relocation, new construction and real estate investments across Miami-Dade, Broward and Palm Beach counties.

Originally from Haiti, Suzana relocated to Miami in 2021, bringing with her a deep appreciation for new beginnings and the opportunities that come with homeownership. Her personal journey has shaped her commitment to helping individuals and families navigate one of life's most important financial decisions with clarity and confidence.

With a background in business, a passion for real estate and hands-on experience guiding clients through a variety of transactions, Suzana combines market knowledge, strategic negotiation and personalized attention to deliver a seamless real estate experience.

Whether assisting a first-time buyer, marketing a property for sale or identifying an investment opportunity, her approach is built on three principles: integrity, transparency and exceptional service.

Fluent in English, French and Haitian Creole, Suzana proudly serves a diverse clientele, building lasting relationships that extend well beyond the closing table.
