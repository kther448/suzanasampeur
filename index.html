Meet Suzana

A personal approach to a major life move.

In 2021, Suzana moved from Haiti to Miami and experienced firsthand what it takes to build a new chapter in a new city. That perspective now shapes the way she guides clients through South Florida real estate: with preparation, patience and a strong understanding of the lifestyle behind every address.

Her work spans first-time homebuyers, relocation, new construction, investment opportunities, sales and rentals. Beyond real estate, Suzana’s public journey includes representing Haiti as Miss World Haiti 2016, community-centered media and public speaking.
